    A token is worth what the market already pays for the same kind of protocol, per dollar of TVL. The model never special-cases a protocol by name.

    StepValueWhat it means

    What is ranked, and what is left out

    • The board ranks every protocol on the snapshot's watchlist, plus every other pre-token protocol the rule keeps — 198 rows in this snapshot, not just the 40 on the watchlist.
    • Left out: wrapped, staked and bridged products, versioned pools, exchange-named protocols, and the bridge, liquid staking, restaking and CEX categories. A launch cannot be scored from a product of a token that already trades.
    • Multiple: the median market-cap-per-TVL of live-token protocols in the same category (at least five of them), otherwise the global median. Clamped to 0.05–3.0 so one outlier cannot set the price.
    • Growth: 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%, at half weight. Maturity: 0.6 at listing, rising to 1.0 over three years; unknown age counts as one year. Breadth: +2% per extra chain, up to five.
    • Implied value is floored at $1. Figures come from the sealed DefiLlama snapshot dated —; nothing is fetched live.

    This is a ranking aid, not investment advice. An implied value is a comparison, not a valuation, and a farm plan is work, not a guarantee of an airdrop.
